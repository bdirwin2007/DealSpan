The problem

Every valuation is only as good as the earnings you feed it.

Small businesses sell for a multiple of earnings. When a seller labels a yearly repair "one-time," adds back a family member who still has to be replaced, or counts savings that never happened, every dollar of fake earnings inflates the price by $2–4. It also inflates the loan the business has to carry.

How it works

Four steps. Every number traced to a source.

Your company

Revenue, EBITDA, cash and current debt, so we can test the combined business.

02

The target

Asking price, three years of numbers from the listing, tax returns, P&L and bank statements, plus the seller's add-backs.

03

The questions

Owner dependence, key employees, recurring revenue, leases, licenses, and why they're selling.

04

The report

Verified earnings, four valuation methods, SBA loan capacity, synergies and a clear offer price.
